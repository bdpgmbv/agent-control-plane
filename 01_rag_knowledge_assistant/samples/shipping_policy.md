# Shipping Options

Standard delivery takes five working days and is free on orders above 50 dollars.
Orders below 50 dollars pay a flat standard shipping fee of 4.99 dollars.

Express delivery takes two working days and costs 12.99 dollars regardless of the
order value.

Same day delivery is available in London and Manchester only, for orders placed
before 11am, and costs 19.99 dollars.

# International Shipping

We ship to 34 countries. International delivery takes 7 to 21 working days
depending on the destination and on local customs processing.

Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer and are
not included in the shipping price.

# Lost Parcels

A parcel is treated as lost after 10 working days past its latest estimated
delivery date. We then either resend the order or issue a full refund, at the
customer's choice.
