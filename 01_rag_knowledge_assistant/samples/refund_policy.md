# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Requests made
after 30 days are declined automatically by the billing system.

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a
different card or to store credit unless the original card has expired.

The original shipping charge is not refunded. If the return is caused by our own
error, for example the wrong item was shipped, we refund the shipping charge as well.

# Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on the
customer's statement. The delay is controlled by the customer's bank, not by us.

# Non Refundable Items

Digital gift cards, personalised engraved items and opened software licences are
not refundable under any circumstances.
