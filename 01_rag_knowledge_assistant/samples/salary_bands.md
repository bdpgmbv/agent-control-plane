# Engineering Salary Bands

Engineer level one earns between 55000 and 70000. Engineer level two earns between
70000 and 90000. Engineer level three earns between 90000 and 120000.

Staff engineers earn between 120000 and 150000. Principal engineers earn between
150000 and 185000.

# Bonus Policy

The annual company bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for individual
contributors and 15 percent for managers. Payment is made in March.
