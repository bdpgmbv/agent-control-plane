    What happens when you press Research

    1. Plan — split the question into independent sub-questions
    2. Validate — drop near-duplicates and any that just restate the question
    3. Research — workers run in parallel, all spending one budget
    4. Extract — a claim plus the quote that supports it; quotes are verified
    5. Deduplicate — collapse findings that say the same thing
    6. Conflicts — find findings that disagree, and keep both
    7. Synthesise — sections, then the summary, from the reserved budget
    8. Verify — strip invented citations, check every figure

    The budget is shared

    One budget for the whole run, not one per worker. Four workers with a quarter each would waste whatever the easy sub-questions do not use, and starve the hard one that needed it.
